About

An unofficial fan tool: what it is, where the data comes from, and how to reach its maintainer.

What this is

An unofficial, fan-made list builder and reference for the Adeptus Custodes in Warhammer 40,000, 11th edition. It is free, has no account and no advertising, and is not a commercial product.

Report an error or ask for removal

Found a wrong value, a missing rule or something that should not be here? Open an issue on GitHub, quoting the page and the item. Rights holders can use the same channel to ask for any content to be changed or removed; requests are handled promptly.

Data status

Last data check: 9 October 2026. Points come from the V2 points table; the table itself is undated, so any later dataslate may differ. Items still to be confirmed against the official sources are marked “to verify” and listed on the Data notes page: Eagle’s Eye wording, Contemptor-Galatus (220 or 230), Prosecutors at 5 models, Venatari with lances, Anathema Psykana Rhino points, Vigilators and Witchseekers intermediate sizes, the Leonine Ferocity / Fierce Conqueror name, and the disposition of Lions of the Emperor.

Third-party commentary

The Art of War and Meta pages paraphrase opinions from third-party video reviews, credited on each page. They are opinion, not rules. Tournament statistics are not republished and no player names are stored.

Privacy

No analytics, no cookies. Your rosters stay in your own browser (local storage). Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when a page opens.
