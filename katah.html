Ka’tah

✦

Custodian units become readied at the start of the Command phase. A readied unit can use one ka’tah when its trigger occurs, then it is no longer readied. Card text follows the Warhammer Community preview; the Favoured line is the Additional Effect printed in the codex for the six Unique Shield Hosts.

Dacatarai

KA’TAH ABILITY
WHEN: Start of the Fight phase.
TARGET: One friendly readied unit.
EFFECT: Your unit’s melee attacks that target an enemy unit (excluding MONSTER/VEHICLE units) can re-roll hit rolls of 1.

Favoured by Dread Host (Purge the Foe): When an enemy unit engaged with your unit makes a pile-in/consolidation move, -2" from that pile-in/consolidation move.

Kaptaris

KA’TAH ABILITY
WHEN: Start of the Fight phase.
TARGET: One friendly readied unit.
EFFECT: Melee attacks that target your unit have -1 to hit rolls.

Favoured by Shadowkeepers (Purge the Foe): You can select one enemy unit engaged with your unit. That enemy unit must make a battle-shock roll, with -1 to that battle-shock roll. You cannot select the same enemy unit for this effect more than once per phase.

Rendax

KA’TAH ABILITY
WHEN: Fight phase, when a friendly readied unit is selected to fight.
TARGET: One friendly readied unit.
EFFECT: Your unit’s melee attacks have [LETHAL HITS: MONSTER/VEHICLE].

Favoured by Emperor’s Chosen (Priority Assets): You can select one enemy MONSTER/VEHICLE unit engaged with your unit and roll one D6: on a 1-2, that enemy unit suffers 1 mortal wound; on a 3-5, D3 mortal wounds; on a 6, 3 mortal wounds.

Conservai

KA’TAH ABILITY
WHEN: Your Command phase.
TARGET: One friendly readied unit.
EFFECT: Until the end of the turn:
  • Being engaged/battle-shocked does not prevent your unit from being eligible to start an action.
  • Starting an action does not prevent your unit from being eligible to shoot.

Favoured by Emissaries Imperatus (Priority Assets): Until the end of the turn, being selected to make an advance/fall-back move does not prevent your unit from being eligible to start an action.

Calistus

KA’TAH ABILITY
WHEN: Your Movement phase, when a friendly readied unit is selected to move.
TARGET: That readied unit.
EFFECT: When your unit makes a normal/advance/fall-back move, your unit can move through all types of model.

Favoured by Solar Watch (Reconnaissance): Your unit has +2" M.

Salvus

KA’TAH ABILITY
WHEN: Your Shooting phase, when a friendly readied unit is selected to shoot.
TARGET: That readied unit.
EFFECT: Your unit can ignore modifiers to your unit’s:
  • BS.
  • Hit rolls and wound rolls.

Favoured by Aquilan Shield (Take and Hold): Your unit’s ranged attacks have +6" R.
