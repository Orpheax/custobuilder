Guardians of the Throne Priority Assets and Purge the Foe

Talon Sortie. When a friendly ADEPTUS CUSTODES unit makes a fall-back move, it can still be eligible to charge.

Open detachment
Aquilan Shield Take and Hold

Gilded Guardians. ADEPTUS CUSTODES unit (not MONSTER/VEHICLE) within range of an objective: ranged attacks targeting it with S greater than its T have -1 to wound.

Open detachment

Instruments of the Emperor’s Wrath. Friendly ADEPTUS CUSTODES units can re-roll charge rolls.

Emissaries Imperatus Priority Assets

Heralds of the Throne. Friendly ADEPTUS CUSTODES units have Fights First.

Open detachment
Emperor’s Chosen Priority Assets

Magna Imperator. When a friendly ADEPTUS CUSTODES unit is selected to attack, its attacks can re-roll one hit roll and one wound roll.

Open detachment
Auric Champions Purge the Foe

Assemblage of Might. In your Command phase, select one enemy unit to be a dreadful foe until your next Command phase. ADEPTUS CUSTODES CHARACTER attacks against it have +1 to wound.

Open detachment
Honoured Companions Take and Hold

Companion’s Watch. TRUSTED SENTINEL unit within range of an objective: its attacks can re-roll wound rolls of 1.

Open detachment
Lions of the Emperor Disruption

On Gilded Wings. At the end of your opponent’s Fight phase, an unengaged ADEPTUS CUSTODES TERMINATOR unit can be placed in strategic reserves.

Open detachment
Might of the Moritoi Take and Hold

Moritoi Ancients. DREADNOUGHT at starting strength: +2 OC. Below starting strength: re-roll hit rolls of 1. Below half-strength: re-roll hit and wound rolls of 1.

Open detachment
Grav-Assault Force Reconnaissance

Flare Shields. Friendly GRAV-ASSAULT units have 4+ InSv against ranged attacks.

Open detachment
Null Maiden Vigil Disruption

Silent Sisterhood. You can select a friendly ANATHEMA PSYKANA CHARACTER as your WARLORD. Friendly PROSECUTOR SQUAD units have BATTLELINE. Friendly ANATHEMA PSYKANA units have Creeping Dread (aura): enemy PSYKER/below-starting-strength units within 12" take battle-shock with -1.

Open detachment

Ka’tah

The six ka’tahs and the detachment that favours each one: Dacatarai (Dread Host), Kaptaris (Shadowkeepers), Rendax (Emperor’s Chosen), Conservai (Emissaries Imperatus), Calistus (Solar Watch), Salvus (Aquilan Shield).
