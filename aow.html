Detachments

Guardians of the Throne

Detachment3 DPGood ▶ 15:58

Presented as the plug-and-play 3 DP option and a good first detachment, with a well-balanced stratagem suite and no dead cards.

What they liked

Sustained + Lethal Hits detachment rule stacks well with the many reroll-ones sources; called the best damage buff in the book alongside standing next to Trajann.

Eagle’s Eye (extra wound + once-per-battle 3+ invulnerable at the moment of allocation) is singled out as the best enhancement; a Shield-Captain on foot with minus-1 damage, 9+ wounds and that invulnerable is very hard to kill.

Unlimited Endurance gives army-wide advance-and-shoot-and-charge, timed after the advance roll.

Swift is the Eagle (reactive D3+3 move, Trusted Sentinel units only) described as their favourite stratagem; superhuman focus re-readies units; Prime Target suits grav tanks.

Concerns

Shield of Honor is dismissed: it only works if the enemy model splits its attacks, which a savvy opponent will never do.

Castellan’s Mark redeploy is nice but weaker than the old turn-order version.

Gives up a slot in a points-hungry army: at 3 DP it competes with building from several 1 DP detachments.

Note: Their advice: the best way to learn what the army does; the later 1 DP combinations are where theorycraft happens.

Shadowkeepers

Unique Shield Host1 DPTop pick ▶ 56:06

The classic anti-melee host: layers of small modifiers to blunt enemy damage, then turns aggressive when it wants to lock opponents down.

What they liked

Objective-linked minus 1 to wound against attacks with Strength above Toughness – effective because the dangerous attackers are the ones that would otherwise wound on 3s/4s.

Kaptaris favoured ka’tah (minus 1 to hit, plus a battle-shock test at minus 1 at start of the fight phase) called possibly the best ka’tah, and the timing denies the opponent offensive stratagems.

No Escape (2 CP) lets a unit charge after an enemy falls back; Indomitable Guardians gives a 1 CP counter-offensive.

Unstoppable Destroyer enhancement is described as a functional fall-back-and-act via objective consolidation.

Concerns

The consolidate-to-leave trick is an enhancement, which is a heavy price for a niche play; lethal-hits stratagem only hits characters/monsters (they wish it said vehicles).

Note: Listed among the four Shield Hosts to start with; they joke that Shadowkeepers + Dread Host together would be the ultimate melee shutdown if only both were allowed.

Solar Watch

Unique Shield Host1 DPTop pick ▶ 1:01:43

The reviewer’s personal favourite and the one he plans to play first: speed turns a traditionally slow army into one of the fastest in the game.

What they liked

Fall back and still charge, army-wide (their comparison point: Emperor’s Children-style mobility).

Calistus favoured ka’tah (+2 Move and move through models) – the one ka’tah that really shines here.

Sally Forth (Shield-Captain only): point-and-click advance and charge for a unit within 6″, no CP and not limited to its own unit; worked example of an infantry unit reaching a 23″ charge.

Venatari with the speed upgrade can threaten a deployment zone on turn one.

Concerns

The upgrade excludes Terminators and the stratagems are mostly minor (Inexorable and Gravimetric Grenade described as corner cases).

Wants a Shield-Captain slot, which competes with Trajann.

Note: Ranked first of the six Shield Hosts by one of the two reviewers; his motto is that speed wins games.

Aquilan Shield

Unique Shield Host1 DPWeak ▶ 32:06

Their pick for the weakest Shield Host: a gun-focused host in an army whose guns are not its strength.

What they liked

Gilded Guardians gives minus 1 to wound against higher-Strength ranged attacks when on objectives – nice against shooters who must come close.

Salvus gets +6″ range, effectively +3″ on rapid fire.

Rapid Reactions (shoot a unit that falls back) and Tip of the Talon (+1 Strength within 9″) are fun.

Concerns

Caduceus Tricks only heals one model; the melta-spear Shield-Captain idea is rejected (two melta shots for 100+ points).

Not as synergistic and gives no real upside, so it is the one they would rank last.

Note: Likely the worst of the six, in their view.

Dread Host

Unique Shield Host1 DPGood ▶ 39:48

Top-three Shield Host in their eyes: reroll charges across the army plus the best-regarded favourite ka’tah effect.

What they liked

Reroll charge rolls for every unit – a big consistency and CP-economy gain for a hyper-elite army where a failed charge can lose a third of your damage.

Dacatarai favoured ka’tah: enemy pile-ins/consolidations are cut by 2″, which freezes opponents who touch you; rated better than the +2 Move option.

A 1 CP advance-and-charge stratagem is included; the two enhancements add Cleave 1 or Sustained Hits 1 to a single character, which they call modest, solid upgrades.

Concerns

Golden Light of the Moirai (2 CP, minus 1 to hit after ingress) is considered over-priced and effectively unusable; Lightning Wrath’s D3+3 pile-in rarely matters on 3-model units.

Note: Synergy flagged with Lions of the Emperor: rerolled 7″ charges from deep strike out of the up-down.

Emissaries Imperatus

Unique Shield Host1 DPSituational ▶ 44:31

Lower half of the Shield Hosts: strong-sounding Fight First rule, but too many of the pieces buff what you are already doing.

What they liked

Fight First for all Custodes models is flagged as strong in melee matchups (though other sources of interrupt/heroic exist).

Edge of the Blade (reroll 1s on charge) is better than the other enhancement.

Slayers of Nightmares (plus 1 to wound vs higher Toughness) fixes the wound roll where Custodes need it – vehicles.

Concerns

Conservai (favourite ka’tah) described as among the least impactful; the shooting/blinding-light enhancement is more of a joke than a threat.

Selfless Service (heroic-style charge for 1 CP) is only a modest CP discount; overall not very sauced up against the competition.

Note: The Shield Host slot is the most contested place in the book, and this one does not win it.

Emperor’s Chosen

Unique Shield Host1 DPTop pick ▶ 51:15

Described as competing for best Shield Host: free reroll-one-hit/reroll-one-wound on everything, strong enhancements and two imported 3 DP stratagems.

What they liked

Built-in rerolls make grav tanks (low shot count, high quality shots) work – their key reason to field Caladius Annihilators here.

Rendax favoured ka’tah adds mortal wounds when you fight monsters/vehicles with lethal hits.

Radiant Mantle (minus 1 to hit, all attacks) lets you spend ka’tah offensively; Hall of Armouries adds Devastating Wounds on melee.

Superhuman Focus, Armour of Contempt and Impenetrable Bastion (sticky objective) all come with it.

Concerns

Radiant Mantle makes the second enhancement a hard choice; the other enhancement has to compete with it.

Note: The reviewer’s second favourite Shield Host; Annihilator tanks only really work with these double rerolls.

Auric Champions

Detachment1 DPSituational ▶ 1:09:18

A character-support detachment: one Dreadful Foe token gives your characters +1 to wound, which only matters if you field 2–3 characters.

What they liked

Duty Unto Death (fight-on-death on 3+, 2+ for characters) is described as the real reason to take it – a source of melee control found nowhere else in the book.

Gilded Champion lets Trajann use Moment Shackle twice and resets the bike captain’s fight-and-fade.

Shroud of the Hidden Blade is a natural home for a solo bike Shield-Captain.

Concerns

The other enhancements (Inspirational Exemplar, Superior Creation) lose out to stronger options in the book; character count in the army is naturally low.

Note: Picked up value with Trajann and the bike captain, not as a generic pick.

Honoured Companions

Detachment1 DPTop pick ▶ 1:13:42

Called the most generically useful of the non-Host detachments: they expect it to see the most play and even say it could stand beside the Shield Hosts.

What they liked

Reroll 1s to wound for Trusted Sentinel units on objectives, in melee and shooting – incredibly consistent with the stack of other reroll-one-hit sources.

Swift is the Eagle again (8″ reactive move, D3+3) – the stratagem the opponent has to play around; combos with surge-style moves.

Avenge the Fallen (below starting strength, plus 2 attacks on non-characters) and Emperor’s Domain (objective consolidate) are useful; Solaritus Centuries makes heroic interventions cheaper.

Concerns

Mostly valuable if you run lots of Custodian Guard / Wardens / Sentinel Guard; the stratagems do not help Terminators or vehicles.

Note: Gives access to Take and Hold.

Lions of the Emperor

Detachment1 DPSituational ▶ 1:18:36

The Terminator detachment: take it if you play two or more Terminator units, otherwise you can skip it.

What they liked

End-of-fight up-down for unengaged Terminators (they lost it from the datasheet).

Vigil Unending (2 CP, minus 1 damage vs shooting or melee for Terminators) is the big one – the reason to play the detachment.

Fury of the Emperor stacks with Dread Host reroll charges on deep-striking Terminators.

Concerns

Lightning Descent and Leonine Ferocity are low-impact; Terminators are expensive at 90+ per model, so deep-striking them to do small tasks costs a lot.

They remember the old version as overvalued and clunky; unleash the lions has been oversold historically.

Note: Breakpoint they give: 1 unit – optional; 2 units – probably; 3+ – yes.

Might of the Moritoi

Detachment1 DPTop pick ▶ 1:25:55

The Dreadnought detachment, and the reviewer’s personal favourite: as soon as one Dreadnought is in the list you should take it.

What they liked

Plus 2 OC while at starting strength (OC4 Telemon / OC5 with the upgrade), reroll hit 1s below starting strength and reroll hit/wound 1s below half.

Auguries Uplink gives a Dreadnought a 5+ feel-no-pain; the first Telemon should take it (they call it almost a full Katan). Memento Moratoy (+1 attack/Strength/damage) is better on a Galatus than a Telemon.

Honoured Interred aura (reroll 1s to hit for friends within 6″), a stratagem that gives Dreadnoughts Assault and charging after an advance, and Unstoppable Momentum (Mobile). With Solar Watch’s +2 Move a Telemon is described as ~16″ on average and able to charge a Knight.

Concerns

Only one of each upgrade per army, so the benefit tops out around 2–3 Dreadnoughts.

Note: Quoted prices: Telemon about 280, about 320 with the 5+ FNP upgrade.

Grav-Assault Force

Detachment1 DPSituational ▶ 1:33:41

A specialist detachment for the grav vehicles; a fine fourth 1 DP slot if you have no better use.

What they liked

4+ invulnerable against ranged attacks for Grav vehicles (including Coronus and Pallas).

Advanced Stabilizers (assault), Inevitable Annihilation (ignore hit modifiers) and the fall-back-and-shoot upgrade are all good for grav tanks.

Concerns

Only worth taking if you are already playing several grav units; the Victory Before Death sticky-on-death trick is cute but unlikely.

Note: Their recommendation: pick a Shield Host, pair with Honoured Companions, then use the last 1 DP for the package that the list is built around (grav tanks, a Telemon or Terminators).

Null Maiden Vigil

Detachment1 DPSkip ▶ 1:36:40

The Sisters of Silence detachment, which they do not expect to take; the other pages are so full of good rules that this is easy to leave out.

What they liked

Oblivion enhancement is strong on paper, giving +1 to hit and (vs Psykers) +1 to wound on a skirmishing unit.

Chaff Volley (prosecutors, +1 AP for the army against the hit unit) is called legitimate.

Concerns

Anathema Psykana units are not keyworded Adeptus Custodes, so some of the intended synergies (rerolls from Trajann, ka’tah) do not apply.

The detachment rule is at best moderately useful (battle-shock pressure on Psykers).

Note: You probably aren’t taking this detachment.

Datasheets

Trajann Valoris

char265 ptsTop pick ▶ 1:41:07

Back in the lists and competing for the best character; a pile of stats with an army-wide consistency aura.

What they liked

Move 8, T7, 10 wounds, 5+ FNP, Leadership 5, OC2; 6″ aura of rerolls of 1 to hit/wound in shooting and melee.

Moment Shackle once per battle: 9 attacks with the Watcher’s Axe (Cleave, S12 AP3 D4), or a 3+ invulnerable.

Hits vehicles, which the army otherwise struggles to kill; hard to dig out of a unit, so he denies two enemy secondaries (Assassinate and Bring It Down).

Concerns

Lost all sources of Damage 3; the only real reason to skip him is that his cost equals a unit slot.

Their take: Play him in every list until a reason not to appears.

Shield-Captain

char205 (first), ~230 (second) ptsTop pick ▶ 1:46:02

The most improved datasheet; one of only two characters they expect to see regularly.

What they liked

Praesidium Shield gives minus 1 damage permanently – never play him without it.

Readies a unit once per battle round, plus a Surge move of D6+2 after being shot.

Strong enhancement carrier; first copy borderline auto-include.

Concerns

A 3-model guard unit with him exceeds 450 points; second copy is a real question once Trajann is in.

Their take: Take with shield. Paragon Blade (precision, 6 attacks) is the alternative when you drop the shield.

Shield-Captain in Allarus Terminator Armour

char185 ptsWeak ▶ 1:50:29

A very expensive stat stick whose main perk (up-down once per battle) is niche.

What they liked

Move 7, T8; Guardian Spear has Cleave on characters; readies a unit once per battle round.

Concerns

Only worth it if you play one Terminator unit with no Lions detachment and still want the up-down; axes (5 attacks, D4 AP2) lose to spears on attack count.

Their take: Probably leave at home.

Shield-Captain on Dawneagle Jetbike

charGood ▶ 1:52:28

Finally has the same attacks as the other captains.

What they liked

8 attacks with Lance, 10 wounds; once per battle (twice with the stratagem) a free move at end of the fight phase; readies a unit.

Brick option if Trajann is not taken; best home for Shroud of the Hidden Blade.

Concerns

No Cleave.

Their take: Solid solo-skirmisher.

Blade Champion

char175 ptsWeak ▶ 1:53:07

The opposite of the Shield-Captain: a drop-off for the points.

What they liked

Three melee profiles, 5 attacks at S10 with Devastating Wounds; rerolls advance and charge for his unit; +1 to wound vs a chosen enemy.

Concerns

Five attacks is poor; he takes a whole unit slot of points and is not close to worth it.

Their take: Skip.

Knight-Centura

anaSituational ▶ 2:33:04

Mostly the same; only quality-of-life changes.

What they liked

+2 Move for a unit and reroll advance/charge.

Concerns

Melee weapon has Devastating Wounds only against Psykers and anti-Psyker 5+, a downgrade.

Their take: Optional.

Sentinel Guard Sodality

bl80 / model ptsGood ▶ 1:54:38

The sword-and-board guard: the durable option and potentially the backbone of lists.

What they liked

5 wounds, OC3 (OC4 with Vexilla), Leadership-modifier immunity with the Vexilla.

Blade pistol: 4 shots at S5 AP1 D2 (12″) with Close Quarters, which lets them shoot out of combat.

Praesidium Shield: minus 1 to wound from higher Strength attackers.

Concerns

5 attacks at S6 AP2 D2 is the worst melee profile in the army; lethal hits only on non-monster/vehicle objective cases (they wanted it against vehicles). Priced at 80 per model, which they suspect may be too much.

Their take: Likely part of the core with Guard and Wardens.

Custodian Guard

bl80 / model ptsGood ▶ 1:58:19

The spear guard: stronger offence than Sentinel Guard.

What they liked

Guardian Spear: 2 shots S5 AP1 D2 at 24″ rapid fire and Assault; 6 attacks at S8 AP2 D2 in melee.

Sustained Hits vs non-monster/vehicle when the objective rule applies; Vexilla for OC4.

Concerns

Cannot take axes (they would not anyway).

Their take: Part of the same core three.

Custodian Wardens

inf295 first / 325 second (98 per model) ptsSituational ▶ 1:59:48

A very good datasheet at a very big premium.

What they liked

6 wounds each; Living Fortress gives minus 1 AP on all attacks against them.

Same guns as the Guard.

Concerns

Compared to the Guard, only +1 wound for +18 points a model; the second unit costs 30 more and they would never play it. AP help does not stop the big shots, so the unit is black and white vs D6 damage.

Cannot stack with Armour of Contempt (excluded by the stratagem).

Their take: Test one unit; perhaps zero. Two-man plus a character is the only configuration they like.

Venatari Custodians — Kinetic Destroyers

inf255 ptsWeak ▶ 2:08:36

Cool pistol design but all Damage 1.

What they liked

Move 12, T7, 2+/4++, 5 wounds; free up-down at the end of the opponent’s fight; Neutroneum cascade mine.

Concerns

Every attack is Damage 1 – they say it would be a good skirmisher at Damage 2.

Their take: Skip in favour of the Lance version.

Venatari Custodians — Verutum Lances

inf270 ptsTop pick ▶ 2:11:12

The most improved datasheet in their view and the one unit they would nearly auto-include as a one-of.

What they liked

Free recursive up-down; free rapid ingress (can double up); Lance in melee with precision; one 18″ S10 AP2 D3 shot.

Move 12 base, can reach deployment-zone charges with Solar Watch.

Concerns

5 attacks a model is a bit light for the price (one reviewer would not call it auto).

Their take: Play one.

Allarus Custodians

term90 / model ptsGood ▶ 2:03:02

Mid-cost and solid; they expect to like it.

What they liked

Move 7, T8 – a major breakpoint vs plasma/melta wounding on 4s – with 6 wounds; plus 1 to wound against higher Toughness.

Grenade launcher is now a flat 3 shots with Blast (S5 AP1); they describe the spear and axe profiles as good.

Multiple flavours of Terminator and a minus-1-damage stratagem make them strong with Lions of the Emperor.

Concerns

At 90 a model they are mid-cost; the unit competes with Guard for a slot.

Their take: Very happy with these; a Vexilla is an auto-include on any unit now.

Aquilon Custodians

term285 gauntlets / 270 talons ptsWeak ▶ 2:05:51

The new Terminators compete poorly with Allarus.

What they liked

Gauntlets: 5 attacks at S10 AP2 D3 and plus 1 AP vs non-monster/vehicle; Talons: heavy flamers and Sustained Hits 2.

Concerns

Damage 1 melee on Talons and no banner (OC2) on Gauntlets; the Allarus are simply a better package; cannot be taken as a 2-man.

Their take: Skip in favour of Allarus.

Vertus Praetors

mnt220 (110 / model) ptsSituational ▶ 2:20:30

Fine rules, too expensive to skirmish.

What they liked

T8 and 7 wounds; Sustained Hits on charge; Lance built in; Hurricane Bolter 6 shots (S5 AP1 D2) twin-linked.

Concerns

Priced into don’t skirmish territory; the Salvo Launcher has no twin-link; huge models.

Their take: Playable as a one-off with bolters.

Gyrfalcon Jetbike Sodality

mnt260 (130 / model) ptsWeak ▶ 2:21:43

Great profile but priced like a Land Raider.

What they liked

+1 AP on the charge, 5 attacks at S8 AP2 D3 with Lance, Deep Strike keyword.

Concerns

Only two models per unit; at 130 a model they compete with Predators and Land Raiders; the models are huge.

Their take: Rarely.

Contemptor-Galatus Dreadnought

dread220 ptsGood ▶ 2:16:57

A solid skirmish piece.

What they liked

Move 9, T10, 12 wounds; Warblade gun is 4 shots S6 AP1 D2 twin-linked; melee 8 attacks at S10 AP2 D3; minus 1 wound against stronger attackers.

Concerns

At this price they compare badly to similar units from other armies.

Their take: Take with the Moratoy damage upgrade on the first copy.

Contemptor-Achillus Dreadnought

dread220 ptsWeak ▶ 2:17:37

Same price as the Galatus and worse.

What they liked

Dread Spear (5 attacks, Lance) and a second gun option.

Concerns

Competes poorly with the Galatus and does not take the damage upgrade as well.

Their take: Prefer the Galatus; see Might of the Moritoi for the 2–2–1 pattern they mention.

Telemon Heavy Dreadnought

dread280 / ~320 with FNP upgrade ptsTop pick ▶ 2:12:45

The reviewer’s favourite unit in the book.

What they liked

Move 10, T11, 14 wounds, OC4; minus 1 damage against ranged attacks.

Spiculus Launcher 36″, 6 shots, Blast 2; double Caestus fists give 7 attacks at S14 AP3 D4 with twin-link and full rerolls, plus Neutronium Cascade flamers.

With the Moratoy upgrades it becomes a fast, near-unkillable monster killer; can even take down a Knight.

Concerns

Multiple copies are taxed; the other guns (storm cannon, accelerator culverin) are inferior to the fists build.

Their take: Always play the first one with fists; pair with Might of the Moritoi.

Pallas Grav-Attack

veh135 ptsWeak ▶ 2:25:02

All the right rules and none of the damage.

What they liked

Move 12, T9, 10 wounds; Mobile Hunter move; fire and fade.

Concerns

Only two shots of S10 – the unit needs an 8-shot S6 gun; they will not field it.

Their take: Leave at home.

Caladius Grav-Tank

veh230 ptsSituational ▶ 2:29:24

The anti-infantry tank; reasonable.

What they liked

T11, 14 wounds, +1 AP on ranged non-monster/vehicle attacks; accelerator cannon with 4 shots; twin Bolt Cannon.

Concerns

They wish the accelerator cannon were AP2 and had another rule.

Their take: Okay for the price; they dislike the Annihilator comparison.

Caladius Annihilator Grav-Tank

veh250 ptsSituational ▶ 2:30:38

Works only when paired with reroll sources.

What they liked

Carronade: 4 shots S12 AP3 D6+2 with Lethal Hits; reroll 1 hit/wound/damage vs monsters and vehicles.

One of three datasheets that kill big vehicles consistently (the others: Trajann and Telemon).

Concerns

Not twin-linked; without Emperor’s Chosen/Prime Target it misses too much to justify 250.

Their take: Only with Emperor’s Chosen; maybe a Prime Target 3 DP.

Coronus Grav-Carrier

veh220 / 225 third ptsWeak ▶ 2:27:34

Faster, still poor.

What they liked

T12 with 16 wounds; reactive D6 move; a Bolt Cannon that is the only good weapon.

Concerns

Advance then disembark – but they cannot charge; blaze cannon is bad.

Their take: Pants before and still pants.

Prosecutor Squad

ana45 for 4 (+5 for a 5th) ptsTop pick ▶ 2:33:55

Cheapest sister and an auto-include – two copies in every list.

What they liked

Move 7, OC2; Detect ability (+3 detection range); anti-Psyker 4+ rapid-fire gun, now S5 AP1 D1.

Concerns

None called out; the break points at 4 / 5 models matter.

Their take: Auto-include; 4-man squads.

Witchseeker Squad

ana55 for 4 / 60 for 5 ptsTop pick ▶ 2:36:18

Another of the most improved models: your skirmisher into small stuff.

What they liked

Scout 7, Move 7; flamer 3 shots S4 AP1 torrent blast with anti-Psyker 4+; shooting forces battle-shock.

5-man for 60 is worth the five points; 35 shots into a 20-man squad.

Concerns

None significant; no tax on sisters.

Their take: Ideal: one 4-man Prosecutor and three 5-man Witchseekers, around 225 points.

Vigilator Squad

ana50 for 4 ptsWeak ▶ 2:35:05

No longer dev wounds: never getting played.

What they liked

Minus 1 to hit in melee, 3 attacks at S5 AP2 D2.

Concerns

Devastating Wounds only into Psykers; not Adeptus Custodes keyword.

Their take: Skip.

Anathema Psykana Rhino

anaSituational ▶ 2:39:14

Same stats; a few small tweaks.

What they liked

Hunter-killer is D3+3 now; disembark after advance; daughters-of-the-abyss aura.

Concerns

Doesn’t heal any more.

Their take: Optional.
