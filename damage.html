How to read this

Every number below the simulator is an average for one use of one weapon profile, fired by one model: attacks, hit roll, wound roll from Strength against Toughness, the better of armour (reduced by AP) and invulnerable save, then Damage, minus any Damage reduction, capped at the target’s Wounds, reduced by Feel No Pain. No cover, re-rolls, leaders or stratagems are assumed in the tables; the simulator adds them. A modifier to a hit or wound roll is capped at ±1 in total, and a natural 1 always fails. Models in a unit multiply these figures, and the number of each weapon depends on the squad.

Modelled keywords: Torrent, Twin-linked, Sustained Hits, Lethal Hits, Devastating Wounds, Anti-X, Rapid Fire and Melta (at half range), Lance (on a charge). Conditional versions (for example Sustained Hits against non-Monster/Vehicle targets) apply only when the target keywords match. Not modelled: Blast, Cleave, Close-quarters, Precision and One Shot, whose 11th edition effects depend on the situation. Datasheet values come from this site’s own data and carry its “to verify” caveats. The C’tan Shard profile (T11, 3+, 4++, 5+ FNP, 16 Wounds, −1 Damage, 345 points) is from the public 11th edition datasheet on Wahapedia and is shown only as a comparison.

Tanking score

How many hits it takes to destroy the whole unit, against four reference weapons (every hit counted, so hit rolls and range do not matter). It combines Wounds, Toughness, armour, invulnerable save, Feel No Pain and Damage reduction. The score is the geometric mean of the four figures, then divided by points. Datasheets in this site’s data carry no Damage reduction, so a what-if column shows the score with −1 Damage on every unit; the C’tan row already includes its own.
